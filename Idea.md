0. Reverse itinerary planner — suggests reachable destinations from current transport hub — tourists don't know what's actually feasible from where they are

1. Fair-fare estimator
Tourists arrive at a station/site and get quoted an auto/taxi fare with zero way to know if it's fair. The app compares the quoted price against a live reference rate (built from historical trip data + distance/time), and flags it if it's inflated — so the tourist can push back or pick another driver on the spot.

2. Last-mile shuttle matching
Many top destinations (waterfalls, forts, remote temples) have no public transport for the final stretch from the nearest bus/train stop. The app matches tourists heading to the same site around the same time into a shared shuttle/auto, splitting cost and solving the "stranded at the last mile" problem.

3. In-ride SOS
Once in a vehicle, especially at night or in unfamiliar areas, tourists (particularly solo/women travelers) have no safety net. A one-tap SOS shares live location + trip details with an emergency contact and/or local authorities, and logs the driver/vehicle for accountability.

Why this trio works as one pitch: they map to the trip in order — before the ride (don't get scammed) → finding the ride (don't get stranded) → during the ride (stay safe) — so it reads as one coherent transport-safety-and-trust layer, not three disconnected features. And the GenAI piece isn't decorative: fare-anomaly detection and a natural-language safety assistant are real model-driven components a judge can ask you to explain.
